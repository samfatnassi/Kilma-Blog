## The Data Foundation: Filtering the Gaia DR3 Archive

The foundation of any robust AI model is high-quality data. The ESA Gaia Data Release 3 (DR3) is a monumental achievement in modern astronomy, but its sheer size makes it challenging to navigate.

Our team at Kilma Labs began by processing an initial dataset of over **1.8 billion** stellar records. Through rigorous filtering for data quality and relevance, we extracted a high-fidelity, pre-processed core of **170 million stars**. This massive subset was specifically curated for large-scale galactic archaeology, 3D mapping, and training advanced machine learning frameworks.

### The 13 Core Parameters

To understand stellar dynamics, we structured the dataset around 13 fundamental astronomical features that capture astrometry, kinematics, and photometry:

- **source_id** — The unique numerical identifier assigned by the Gaia DR3 mission.
- **ra / dec** — Equatorial Coordinates (Right Ascension $\alpha$ and Declination $\delta$) mapping the star's position in the sky.
- **l / b** — Galactic Coordinates (Longitude $l$ and Latitude $b$) relative to our galaxy's disk alignment.
- **pmra / pmdec** — Proper Motion $(\mu_{\alpha*},\ \mu_{\delta})$, representing the angular velocity of the star across the sky, measured in milliarcseconds per year $(\mathrm{mas\ yr^{-1}})$.
- **d_pc** — The calculated distance from Earth in Parsecs, derived from the inverse of the parallax:

$$
d\,[\mathrm{pc}] = \frac{1000}{\varpi\,[\mathrm{mas}]}
$$

- **x, y, z** — 3D Heliocentric Cartesian mapping, providing the star's position relative to our Sun:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x &= d\cos b\cos l \\
y &= d\cos b\sin l \\
z &= d\sin b
\end{aligned}
$$

- **abs_m** — Absolute Magnitude, which indicates the intrinsic, true brightness of the star:

$$
M = m + 5 - 5\log_{10}\!\left(d\,[\mathrm{pc}]\right)
$$

- **bp_rp** — Color Index, which calculates the difference between the BP and RP light bands, serving as a primary indicator of the star's surface temperature:

$$
\mathrm{BP-RP} = G_{\mathrm{BP}} - G_{\mathrm{RP}}
$$

## The AI Engine: SADIM-77M (Stellar Intelligence Framework)

Processing 170 million stars using traditional tabular database queries is both slow and computationally expensive. To solve this, we developed **SADIM-77M**, a specialized Foundation AI model trained entirely on our refined Gaia DR3 subset.

SADIM (Stellar Intelligence Framework) utilizes a **Variational Autoencoder (VAE)** architecture equipped with **77 million parameters**. Instead of querying a traditional database, SADIM converts raw stellar metadata into a High-Dimensional Neural Vector Space. Every single celestial object is encoded into a **512-dimensional latent vector**:

$$
\begin{aligned}
z &\in \mathbb{R}^{512} \\
z &\sim q_\phi(z \mid x) = \mathcal{N}\!\left(\mu_\phi(x),\ \sigma^2_\phi(x)\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

This vector acts as a unique *"Digital Fingerprint"* for the star, encapsulating its statistical essence. The model is trained by minimizing the standard variational objective — a reconstruction term plus a divergence term that regularizes the latent space:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L} = \;& \underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{q_\phi(z\mid x)}\!\left[\lVert x - \hat{x}\rVert^2\right]}_{\text{reconstruction}} \\[4pt]
+\;& \underbrace{D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(q_\phi(z\mid x)\,\Vert\,p(z)\right)}_{\text{regularization}}
\end{aligned}
$$

By mapping stars in this high-dimensional space, the model achieves several core objectives:

- **Galactic Archaeology** — It easily identifies stellar streams and ancient galactic structures by grouping similar digital fingerprints.
- **Big Data Optimization** — It achieves a **99.9% reduction in query latency** compared to traditional methods.
- **Scalability** — It provides a framework capable of real-time stellar analysis for future space surveys.

**Model Access:** The SADIM-77M model is available for fast inference, predicting missing stellar properties, and automating the classification of new astronomical data: [SADIM-77M on HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/KilmaAI/Sadim-77M).

## Autonomous Discovery: The Extreme Anomalies Catalog

The most powerful application of representing the universe in a neural vector space is **anomaly detection**. By analyzing the latent manifold, SADIM-77M can autonomously identify rare stellar phenomena that deviate from the cosmic norm.

Using the AI engine, we scanned the galaxy and extracted the **SADIM Extreme Anomalies Catalog** — a curated dataset of **10,000 stellar outliers** detected from the Gaia DR3 catalog.

### Dataset Specifications

- **Precision** — Guaranteed 19-digit `source_id` for verified strings.
- **Target** — The catalog isolates hyper-velocity stars, unresolved binary systems, and extreme kinematic anomalies.
- **AI Metrics** — The anomalies are strictly ranked by two AI-specific calculations: **MSE Deviation** (Mean Squared Error) and **KL Surpass** (Kullback–Leibler Divergence), which measure how *"surprised"* the AI is by a star's physical behavior.

$$
\mathrm{MSE} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_i - \hat{x}_i\right)^2
$$

$$
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q \Vert p) = \sum_{i} q(i)\,\log\frac{q(i)}{p(i)}
$$

**Catalog Access:** [SADIM Extreme Anomalies Catalog](https://huggingface.co/datasets/samfatnassi/extreme-anomalies-catalog).

## Interactive Exploration: SADIM Sky Explorer

To bridge the gap between complex AI architecture and practical usability, we built the **SADIM Sky Explorer**, an interactive analytical tool designed for researchers and astronomy enthusiasts alike.

This tool combines real-time data retrieval — powered by DuckDB and the ESA TAP API — with our SADIM-77M deep learning model. Users can input any valid Gaia DR3 `SOURCE_ID` (for example, try: `38655544960`) and click **"SCAN"**. The system instantly plots the star on an interactive deep-space map (via Aladin Lite) and generates a detailed diagnostic report.

### Understanding the Scan Report

The dashboard provides a blend of physical calculations and AI metrics:

- **DIST_LY & PARALLAX** — The estimated distance in Light-Years and the apparent shift against the background, in $\mathrm{mas}$.
- **VEL_KMS (TAN) & RADIAL_VEL** — Tangential and radial speeds in $\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$. The tangential velocity follows

$$
v_{\mathrm{tan}} = 4.74\,\mu\,[\mathrm{mas/yr}] \times d\,[\mathrm{kpc}]
$$

  Values exceeding $600\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ may trigger a hypervelocity alert.

- **RUWE (ESA_API)** — Renormalised Unit Weight Error. A value near $1.0$ is normal; above $1.4$ suggests a poor fit, often indicating an unseen binary companion or noisy data.
- **LOCAL_COUNT** — The number of neighboring stars within the target's immediate spatial cone.
- **MSE_DEV & KL_SURP** — AI metrics indicating how much the star deviates from expected baselines, and how highly unusual its data distribution is.

Based on these metrics, the AI outputs a final `STATUS` classification:

- **[NOMINAL (NORMAL)]** — Standard star.
- **[DATA ARTIFACT: BAD ASTROMETRY (RUWE)]** — Likely a binary system or observation noise.
- **[PHYSICAL ANOMALY: HYPERVELOCITY]** — Moving at extreme speeds.
- **[AI GLOBAL ANOMALY]** — Flagged by the deep learning model due to highly unusual multidimensional patterns.
